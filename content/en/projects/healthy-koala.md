Healthy Koala plans a week of healthy meals in Spanish, using recipes written by
certified nutritionists.

- **Weekly plan, recipe swaps and an automatic grocery list**, plus food equivalences.
- **Koala Bot**, an assistant built on Anthropic's Claude that answers questions about
  the plan.
- **Mobile-first PWA** with light and dark modes. React, TypeScript and Tailwind on the
  front end (TanStack Query and Zustand for state), FastAPI on Python 3.12 behind it,
  Supabase for Postgres and auth, deployed on Vercel and Railway.
